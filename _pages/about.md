# About Me

I am a theoretical chemist interested in understanding excited-state
phenomena in molecular systems using quantum chemical methods.

My research focuses on excited-state electronic structure, molecular
aggregates, excitonic interactions, singlet fission, and energy-transfer
processes. A central theme of my work is developing physically transparent
model Hamiltonians informed by ab initio electronic structure calculations.
